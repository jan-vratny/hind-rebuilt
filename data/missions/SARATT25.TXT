:Title
Autorotation Training
:Description
Helicopters: 1
:Para
Targets: None
:Para
Take-off time: 10:00
:Para
Flight time: 2 mins
:Para
Weather: Clear
:Para
You start at 900 m over the base at 200 km/h, the engines running. Press O: the engines drop to idle and nothing drives the rotor. At once lower the collective to 35 %, or the revolutions fall away in seconds. The HUD now shows the rotor's RPM in place of the torque: keep it at 90-110 %. More collective spends it, less lets it climb; 35-45 % holds it.
:Para
Glide at about 150 km/h, sinking 15-20 m/s: 900 m leaves about a minute and 3 km of ground. Turn back towards the base early and lower the gear (U).
:Para
At 50 m raise the nose to about +20 degrees: the speed and the sink fall and the RPM climbs. At 12 m bring the nose back level and raise the collective as much as it takes to touch down below 4 m/s. You get this one chance: there is no going round. On the wheels lower the collective and brake (B).
:Para
Land anywhere in one piece with the engines disengaged since at least 100 m up. Land within 500 m of the start for an outstanding. O engages the engines again, some seconds to full power, for a try that has gone wrong, but a landing under power fails the drill. Stable or Realistic only: a Novice pilot flies this one in Stable.
:Outstanding
Down on the base without power. That one will save your life.
:Success
Down in one piece without power, though not on the base. Next time turn back earlier.
:Failure
That was no autorotation: the engines have to stay off down to the ground. Fly it again.
