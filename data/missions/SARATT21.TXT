Operational Training - Drone Interception
:Description

:Para
Targets: 3 Shahed-136 drones
:Para
Weapons: Gun, door guns, UPK-23 gun pods
:Para
Take-off time: 11:00
:Para

:Para
Weather: Clear
:Para
Before you fly the nights, learn the hunt by day. Three Shahed-136 drones will come in from the south-east towards our airbase, as a real raid would. The Hip finds them and calls them out ('SHAHED 2 O'CLOCK 4.3 KM LOW'), a diamond on the HUD marks the one called. Turn towards it, close in behind or alongside at its speed (about 180 km/h) and let the gun do the work: the WSO lays it on the drone when it is in reach. The inner pylons carry UPK-23 gun pods for a heavier burst head on. Door gunners down them alongside by themselves; D in the air tells them to hold fire, F4 takes a door gun yourself.
:Para
Shoot down at least 2. If 2 get through, the mission has failed.
:Outstanding
All three down. You are ready for the nights.
:Success
Most of them down. Fly it again until none gets through.
:Failure
Too many got through. Fly it again: listen to the Hip, and get alongside them.
