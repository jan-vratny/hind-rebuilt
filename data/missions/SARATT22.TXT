A daylight swarm close to home, to fly together: twenty-four Shahed-136 drones in four waves of six, with a few Gerbera decoys, all coming in from the south-east at our airbase and the town. The patrol line is only 8 km out, the first wave reaches it some two and a half minutes after take-off and the next follows every two and a half minutes. Split the waves between you, land at the base to rearm when the guns run dry, and get back up.
:Para
Shoot down at least 16. If 6 get through, the mission has failed.
:Outstanding
Not one of the twenty-four got through. Well flown, all of you.
:Success
Most of the swarm went down. Fly it again and get them all.
:Failure
Too many got through. Fly it again: share the waves, and keep the guns fed.
