:Para
The pylons carry two racks of interceptor drones, six on each, as the Mi-8 crews have them. They are the door gunners' to fly: when a jet Geran-3 comes within 5 km they let an interceptor go at it, and it steers itself onto it at some 400 km/h. The slower Shaheds they leave to their guns, unless you tell them (G) to send one at your target. A gunner guiding an interceptor has his hands full: his gun is silent till it is close to its drone. D in the air holds them, as it holds the door guns. Six Shaheds and two Geran-3s will come in at our airbase: take the flight to the patrol line and watch the interceptors work, the gun is yours for what they miss.
:Para
Shoot down at least 5. If 2 get through, the mission has failed.

:Failure
